Support

Write to info@mobirexa.de and we'll get back to you, usually within two working days. Tell us your phone model, the email address of your account and what you tried.

How do I add my partner?

Open Family → Invite an adult, enter their email address and share the invite code with them. They install Famelo, sign up with that same address, confirm it, then choose “I have an invite” and enter the code. The code works once and expires after 24 hours.

Do my kids need an account?

No. Add them under Family → Add child profile. When you add a child, you choose which adults can see and manage their plans. You can change this later in Family → Manage family.

Who can see my appointments?

Each appointment has its own setting:

  • Whole family: every member sees the details.
  • Only me: no one else sees it.
  • Busy only: the others see that you're busy and when, without the title, place or notes.

I don't get notifications

Check that notifications for Famelo are allowed in your phone's settings, and that Focus or Do Not Disturb is not holding them back. Then open Famelo once while online, so your phone is registered again.

I made changes without internet

They are saved on your phone and sent automatically once you're online. If someone changed the same appointment in the meantime, Famelo shows both versions and you pick the one to keep.

Can I get a copy of my data?

Yes. Settings → Export my data creates a file with your profile and the calendar entries you can see.

Delete your account

In the app: Settings → Delete my account. Enter your password and type DELETE to confirm. Your profile, memberships, the appointments you created, your notification tokens and your invitations are deleted right away. A family with no members left is deleted with all its data. Backups are overwritten within 30 days.

No longer have the app? Email info@mobirexa.de from the address you signed up with, with the subject “Delete my Famelo account”. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email. See the privacy policy for details.
