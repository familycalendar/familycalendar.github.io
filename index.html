Everyone's plans in one calendar. You decide who sees what.

Famelo is a shared calendar for parents and kids. Add the dentist, the swimming lesson and the parent-teacher meeting once, and the whole family has them on their phone.

Download on theApp Store Get it onGoogle Play

Your family's day at a glance

Today shows every plan in time order, with what's on now and what's next. Tap a name to see just one person's day, or switch to week, month and agenda.

Pick who each appointment is for

Choose one person or several. Each family member has a color, so a glance at the calendar tells you who has to be where. Everyone gets a reminder before their own appointments, and a notification when someone adds one for them.

Kids don't need an account

Add your children as profiles in a few seconds. You choose which adults can see and manage each child's plans. Invite your partner by email, and you are set up.

Works without a connection

Plans you add on the train or in a basement are sent once you're back online. If the same appointment changed on two phones, you pick the version to keep.

No ads, no tracking

Your family's plans are not for sale. Export your data or delete your account at any time, right in the app.

English, German and Turkish

Famelo follows your phone's language. Spanish, French, Portuguese and Italian are coming in the next update.
