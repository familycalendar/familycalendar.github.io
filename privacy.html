Famelo

Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Famelo is a shared calendar for families. This policy explains which personal data the Famelo app and this website process, why, where, and what you can do about it. We keep it short because we collect little: no ads, no analytics, no tracking.

Who we are

Famelo is published by Mobirexa, an individual developer based in Germany (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described here. Contact for anything related to privacy: info@mobirexa.de.

What we process

Your account

Your email address, a password (stored only as a hash by our sign-in provider), your display name, language and time zone. We need these to create your account, sign you in and show dates correctly.

Your family and calendar

  • The family you create or join: its name, its members and their roles.
  • Child profiles you add: a name and color. Children do not get an account and do not use Famelo themselves.
  • Which adults may see and manage a child's plans.
  • Appointments: title, start and end time, place, notes, who it is for, who added it, and who can see it.
  • Invitations: the email address of the person you invite.

Who in your family sees an appointment is up to you. With “Only me” no one else sees it. With “Busy only” the others see only that you are busy and when, not the title, place or notes.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store a push token for your device so we can send reminders and tell you when someone adds an appointment for you. The notification text is general, for example “A new appointment was added for you”. It contains no appointment details.

Data on your phone

The app keeps a copy of your family's calendar on your phone so it works offline, and stores your sign-in session in the system keychain. Changes you make offline wait on the phone until they are sent.

What we do not collect

No location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or usage analytics. The app contains no advertising or tracking code, and we do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Why and on which legal basis

  • To provide Famelo as you asked: your account, your family's shared calendar, syncing between devices and notifications (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR, performance of a contract).
  • To keep the service secure and working, for example to stop abuse and fix errors (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, our legitimate interest in a reliable service).
  • To answer you when you write to us (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

Data about children is entered by their parents or guardians, who decide what to add. We use it only to show the family's calendar.

How long we keep data

  • Your account, family and appointments: until you delete them or your account.
  • When you delete your account, we delete your profile, your memberships, the appointments you created, your push tokens and your invitations right away. A family that has no members left is deleted with all its data.
  • Our database provider keeps backups for disaster recovery. Deleted data disappears from them when they rotate, at the latest after 30 days.
  • Push tokens: until you sign out, turn notifications off or delete your account. Expired tokens are removed when Apple or Google report them invalid.
  • Emails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, then deleted.

Your rights

You can, at any time:

  • Export your data as a file: Settings → Export my data.
  • Delete your account and data: Settings → Delete my account. You can also ask us to delete it.
  • Correct your name and appointments in the app.

Under the GDPR you also have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object to processing based on legitimate interest. To use these rights, email info@mobirexa.de from the address linked to your account. We answer within one month. You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, for example the one where you live.

Age

You must be at least 16 to create an account. Children are added as profiles by a parent or guardian and do not sign in.

Security

Data travels encrypted (TLS). Access in the database is limited per family by row-level security rules, so a user can only read what their family shares with them. Passwords are never stored in plain text.

This website

This website sets no cookies and loads no third-party scripts or fonts. GitHub Pages, which hosts it, processes your IP address and browser details in its server logs to deliver the pages and protect them from abuse (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See GitHub's privacy statement.

Changes

If we change this policy, we update this page and the effective date. If a change affects how we use your data, we tell you in the app before it takes effect.
